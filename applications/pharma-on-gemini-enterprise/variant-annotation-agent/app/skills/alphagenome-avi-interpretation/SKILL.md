# AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) Interpretation

This skill provides an authoritative methodology for evaluating deep learning variant effect predictions generated by Google DeepMind's AlphaGenome foundation model.

## Score Definitions & Scaling

AlphaGenome produces multimodal functional predictions across 18 biological tracks. The overall **AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI)** summarizes genome-wide functional disruption:

1. **AVI Raw (`avi_raw`)**:
   - Aggregate absolute feature divergence across 18 experimental and regulatory tracks.
2. **AVI Quantile (`avi_quantile`)**:
   - Empirical genome-wide percentile ranking ($0.0 \le Q \le 1.0$) calibrated against 3 billion human genomic positions.
3. **AVI Phred (`avi_phred`)**:
   - Standardized log-scaled impact metric defined as:
     $$\text{AVI}_{\text{phred}} = -10 \cdot \log_{10}(1 - \text{quantile})$$
   - Matches clinical convention used by CADD and PolyPhen-2.

## Phred Impact Tiering

| Phred Score | Quantile Range | Tier Classification | Clinical / Biological Actionability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$\ge 20.0$** | $\ge 99.0\text{th}$ | **High Impact** | Strong evidence of molecular perturbation; top 1% genome-wide impact. High priority for ACMG PP3 evidence. |
| **$15.0\text{ – }19.99$** | $96.8\text{th – }98.9\text{th}$ | **Moderate-High Impact** | Significant regulatory or splicing alteration; top 3% genome-wide impact. |
| **$10.0\text{ – }14.99$** | $90.0\text{th – }96.7\text{th}$ | **Moderate Impact** | Noticeable biological effect in specific cell types or assays; top 10% genome-wide impact. |
| **$< 10.0$** | $< 90.0\text{th}$ | **Low / Baseline Impact** | Neutral or minimal functional effect; consistent with background genomic variation. |

## 18 Evaluated AlphaGenome Modalities

1. `MERGED_SPLICING`: Cryptic splice site activation, exon skipping, or splice acceptor/donor disruption.
2. `MAX_ABS_RNA_SEQ`: Quantitative change in transcript abundance (log2FC).
3. `MAX_ABS_ATAC`: Transposase-accessible chromatin alteration.
4. `MAX_ABS_DNASE`: DNase I hypersensitive site accessibility change.
5. `MAX_ABS_CHIP_TF`: Disruption or gain of transcription factor binding peaks.
6. `MAX_ABS_CHIP_HISTONE`: Epigenetic histone modification changes (e.g. H3K4me3, H3K27ac, H3K27me3).
7. `MAX_ABS_CAGE`: Cap analysis gene expression (transcription start site activity).
8. `MAX_ABS_PROCAP`: Precision run-on transcription initiation alteration.
9. `MAX_ABS_POLYADENYLATION`: Cleavage and polyadenylation signal disruption.
10. `MAX_ABS_CONTACT_MAPS`: 3D chromatin conformation (Hi-C / Micro-C loop contact disruption).
11. `ALPHAMISSENSE`: Missense protein pathogenicity likelihood.
12. `CACTUS_241_WAY`: 241-way mammalian genome alignment conservation.
13. `PROTEIN_TERMINATION`: Premature truncation or extension of open reading frame.
14. `START_LOST`: Disruption of canonical translation initiation codon.
15. `STOP_LOST`: Extension of peptide sequence past stop codon.
16. `PHASTCONS_470_WAY`: Vertebrate phylogenetic conservation score.
17. `IS_INSERTION`: Small insertion disruption flag.
18. `IS_DELETION`: Small deletion disruption flag.

## AlphaGenome Atlas Linking Protocol

Always provide direct exploration URLs for clinical reviewers:
- **Variant View**: `https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?variant={chr}:{pos}:{ref}>{alt}`
- **Locus View**: `https://deepmind.google.com/science/alphagenome/atlas?locus={chr}:{start}-{end}`
