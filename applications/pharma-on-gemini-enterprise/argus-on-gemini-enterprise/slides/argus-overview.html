The challenge

Diligence is slow, fragmented, and evidence-heavy

Evaluating an acquisition target means reconciling the science, the competition, the clinic, the regulator, and the balance sheet — each living in a different corpus, each requiring a specialist to read and cite.
Scattered evidence

Many corpora, one question

Papers, trials, regulatory reviews, protein data, and filings must be pulled together and cross-checked by hand.

Analyst time

Weeks per target

Scientific PoS, competitive landscape, and a defensible valuation each take days of specialist effort.

Traceability

Every figure must cite

Investment decisions demand sourced, verifiable claims — not unattributed model output.

The solution

One agent, three modes of diligence

Mode 1 · Seconds

Quick question

A fast, sourced answer on a company, asset, trial, regulatory status, or financials — with citations.

Mode 2 · Ranked

Target screening

A ranked shortlist of acquisition candidates from a thesis — therapeutic area, modality, stage, budget — with strategic fit and key risk for each.

Mode 3 · Minutes

Detailed whitepaper

An investment-grade assessment as a downloadable PDF with data charts and a competitive infographic, plus an inline executive summary.

Six specialist analysts — scientific PoS, competitive, clinical/regulatory, commercial, financial/deal, and IP — run concurrently, then a synthesizer composes the report.
